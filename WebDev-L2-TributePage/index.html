Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India and the People's President

About Dr. Kalam

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 and passed away on 27 July 2015. He was an Indian aerospace engineer, scientist, and science administrator.

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He played an important role in India's civilian space programme and military missile development efforts.

Dr. Kalam is popularly known as the “Missile Man of India” for his contribution to the development of ballistic missile and launch vehicle technology.

Early Life and Education

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a modest family. His father was a boat owner and imam, while his mother was a homemaker. From an early age, Kalam was known for his curiosity, discipline, and strong interest in learning.

He studied physics at Saint Joseph's College in Tiruchirappalli and later specialised in aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology. His passion for aviation and space technology shaped his future career as a scientist.

Career as a Scientist

After graduating from the Madras Institute of Technology in 1960, Kalam joined the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) as a scientist.

He later joined the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), where he became the project director of India's first satellite launch vehicle, SLV-3. The vehicle successfully deployed the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit in July 1980.

Kalam played a major role in the development of missiles including Agni and Prithvi. His leadership and scientific achievements brought him national recognition and inspired generations of students and scientists.

He also played an important role in India's Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998 and contributed significantly to the modernisation of India's defence technology.

Presidency

Dr. Kalam was elected as the 11th President of India in 2002 and served until 2007. He was widely respected for his humility, integrity, and dedication to public service.

During his presidency, he focused on promoting education, science, and technology, and he encouraged young people to dream big and work hard to achieve their goals.

Dr. Kalam's tenure as President was marked by his efforts to connect with the youth of India, and he became known as the "People's President" for his approachable and inspiring nature.

Author and Teacher

Dr. Kalam was also a respected author. His books, including Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020, inspired millions of readers, especially students and young professionals.

After completing his presidential term, he continued to teach and interact with students across India. He believed that education, innovation, and hard work were essential for the progress of the nation.

Vision for India

Dr. Kalam imagined India as a developed, self-reliant, and technologically advanced nation. Through his vision of India 2020, he encouraged citizens to work together in the fields of education, healthcare, agriculture, science, and technology.

He believed that young people had the power to transform society. His simple lifestyle, positive attitude, and dedication to public service made him one of India's most admired leaders.

Famous Quote

“Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action.”
— Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Legacy

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam continues to be remembered as a great scientist, inspiring teacher, visionary leader, and role model. His life and words continue to motivate young people to dream big and work hard to build a better future.
